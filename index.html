How IndustryToSchools Works

Connecting education and enterprise in three straightforward steps.

01

Connect

District coordinators post classroom requirements, or corporate partners list available internships, guest speaker modules, and equipment donations.

02

Match & Verify

Automated matching pairs resources based on technical field, geographic location, and verified compliance or background clearance requirements.

03

Impact

Students gain hands-on career experience, industrial equipment is deployed into active classrooms, and real-time reporting is generated for district stakeholders.
